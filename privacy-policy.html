Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Search Puzzle ("the App") handles information when you use the application.

Word Search Puzzle is a casual word puzzle game developed by Jai Bhagwan Sharma.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account, provide your name, phone number, postal address, or other personal information to play.

The App may process certain technical information automatically through third-party services used to provide advertising and improve the reliability of the App.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising information, and information about interactions with advertisements, subject to Google's policies and your device settings.

Google may use this information to provide, measure, and personalize advertising where permitted by applicable law and user settings.

For more information about how Google handles data, please visit: Google Privacy Policy

3. Internet Connection

The App requires an active internet connection to start and play gameplay. Internet connectivity is also required for certain third-party services, including advertising.

The App may check the availability of the network connection to determine whether gameplay can be started and whether online services are available.

4. Local Data Storage

The App stores certain game-related information locally on your device, such as game progress, unlocked levels, scores, best times, statistics, settings, and other gameplay preferences.

This information is used to provide game functionality and is not intended to identify you personally.

5. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services such as Google AdMob for advertising. These third-party services may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing Google's privacy policy for information about Google's data practices.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices

You can control certain advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and your Google account settings. Depending on your location, applicable privacy choices may also be presented through Google's advertising services.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
